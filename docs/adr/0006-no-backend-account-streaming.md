# ADR-0006: No backend, account or streaming in MVP v1.0

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-07-27
- **Authority:** [PRD v1.0](../PRD.md) and [MVP v1.0](../MVP.md)
- **Approval:** Pending in [Issue #2](https://github.com/latticeward-labs/music-copilot/issues/2)

## Proposed decision

MVP v1.0 has no account, authentication, backend, cloud sync, online catalog, streaming, download, upload or content distribution. The app plays lawful audio already present on the user's device.

After successful Beta evidence, Discovery may examine one legal music-service connection and iOS. Neither is committed before the decision gate.
