## Failure scenarios and limitations

1. **Deleted or stale selected MediaStore file before selection:** existing P03 resolver rejects selection without committing a fake playing queue; Library/Search remains accessible. M29 **NOT_RUN**.
2. **Truncated/malformed decoder content mid-play:** Media3's categorized error triggers safe pause and up to 3 later revalidated occurrences; no unbounded loop even if consecutive successors also fail. M30 **NOT_RUN**.
3. **Permission withdrawn while active or storage ejected:** `ERROR_CODE_IO_NO_PERMISSION` or bounded-depth SecurityException becomes `PERMISSION_REVOKED`; invalidate resolver and show permission-recovery copy. Missing file/removed volume becomes a bounded missing-media case. Unknown OEM IO failures safe-pause rather than assuming corruption. M31 **NOT_RUN**.
4. **Manual commands & concurrent IO:** generation guard cancels effects of stale auto-recovery after manual Pause, Stop, ReplaceQueue, Next or route loss; exact selected queue revision + URI revalidated before seek/prepare. No second player and no changes to user's media files.
5. **No available successor, last queue position, or 3-attempt cap:** never loop to beginning, automatically repeat, delete/copy file or claim audio playing; stay safe-paused with typed error and manual Library navigation.
6. **Known P12 integrated Android verification needs:** last-error transition timing from Media3 (onPlayerError occurs before STATE_IDLE), restore of real audio with `seekTo/prepare` after decoder failure, reentrancy/revision checks, duplicate-ID queue handling, OEM permission-revocation classifications, very large queue/10-second controller timeout, Play after Settings, accuracy of no-file-changes claim, and 150/200% accessible recovery screen. Source review is NOT Android or device proof. The product risk remains until the final integrated APK is actually built and used for M29–M31.
